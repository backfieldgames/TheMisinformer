Staring at sun helps eye health?

A new and highly questionable study suggests that staring directly into the sun might improve eye health — or at least “toughen you up.”

“We found that people who squint harder tend to blink more efficiently,” said Dr. Stan Lightman of the Totally Real Weather Institute. “That’s a win in our book.”

In fact, many test subjects were reportedly empowered by their sun-gazing experience, describing it as “a moment of clarity followed by sudden darkness and mild screaming.”

Parents are being warned not to try this at home — especially since the study’s only published results were found scribbled in crayon next to a melted pair of sunglasses.

Meanwhile, the Teletubbies’ iconic Sun Baby has endorsed the study, stating, “Goo goo ga ga. Vitamin D supremacy!”

NASA and the American Optometric Association both released a joint statement reading, simply: “Please stop.”
