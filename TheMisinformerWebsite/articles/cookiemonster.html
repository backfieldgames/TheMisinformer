Cookie Monster says cookies are good for you

In a shocking announcement broadcasted worldwide, Cookie Monster, the universally adored Sesame Street resident and cookie connoisseur, has declared that "cookies are officially a health food."

Speaking from his new role as “Director of Snack-Based Wellness” at the United Nations, Cookie Monster outlined a five-point nutrition plan which heavily, and suspiciously, favors cookies in every meal group.

“Breakfast? Cookie. Lunch? Cookie. Dinner? BIG cookie,” Cookie Monster stated at the Geneva summit, showering crumbs across several horrified diplomats.

While health experts were quick to disagree, Cookie Monster cited a little-known study he found “on the back of a chocolate chip bag” as proof. “Me have evidence! Big evidence! And chocolate chip pieces!” he proclaimed.

In response to the controversy, the WHO (World Health Organization) issued a clarification: "No, cookies are not a vegetable."

Still, Cookie Monster’s message has caught on with millions, leading to skyrocketing cookie sales, global frosting shortages, and a new fitness trend called "Cookie Yoga," where participants balance Oreos on their heads while chanting, "Om nom nom."

As Cookie Monster himself summed it up: “Me no doctor...but me happy. And happiness healthy, right?”
