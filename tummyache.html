Man sad because of tummy ache

Local man Jared Cluff, 27, is reportedly “not doing so hot” after consuming four gas station burritos and two chili cheese dogs in under ten minutes — a decision that has led to what doctors describe as “tummy devastation.”

“He said he was fine,” reported his friend Kyle. “Then he sat down and made a noise that sounded like a kettle slowly dying.”

Emergency services were not contacted, but several blankets and a SpongeBob-shaped ice pack were deployed. “He didn’t want water,” Kyle added. “He wanted a second chili cheese dog. This may be a cry for help.”

Medical professionals recommend avoiding any food labeled “volcano” or “risky midnight snack” during weekdays. “A tummy ache isn’t just physical,” said one gastroenterologist. “It’s a spiritual reckoning.”

As of press time, Jared was seen curled in a blanket watching *Shrek 2* and whispering, “This is my swamp now.”
