Florida woman calls 911 after McDonald's doesn't have Chicken Nuggets

In a truly bizarre fast-food fiasco, a Florida woman dialed 911 when she discovered her local McDonald's was completely out of Chicken Nuggets.

The call, which quickly went viral, left emergency dispatchers confused — and hungry. “They said they had no McNuggets,” the woman cried on the phone. “And that’s not right. That’s not right!”

Police responded to the scene, not to arrest anyone at McDonald's — but to issue a stern warning to the woman about misuse of emergency services. Despite the frustration, officers assured the public that nugget justice would prevail.

This isn't the first time fast food has driven someone to the edge in Florida, and it likely won't be the last.
