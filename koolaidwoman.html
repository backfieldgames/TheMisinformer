Woman falls in love with Kool Aid Man

In what might be the boldest romance since Shrek and Fiona, a Wisconsin woman has professed her love for none other than the Kool Aid Man — the bright red pitcher known for busting through walls and quenching thirsts nationwide.

“I don’t care if he’s 85% corn syrup and 15% glass shards,” said 34-year-old Marissa Plunkett. “When he says 'Oh Yeah!'... I feel seen.”

The two reportedly met at a Fourth of July barbecue when the Kool Aid Man crashed — literally — through her cousin's garage. “Most people were screaming,” she said, “but I saw a gentle sweetness behind those cherry-red eyes.”

Since then, the couple has been spotted on beach walks, date nights at Denny’s, and even attending couples counseling — where Kool Aid Man mostly just said "OH YEAHHHHH!" in various tones of agreement.

Critics have called the relationship unstable, citing "high sugar content and a tendency to shatter under pressure." But Marissa remains undeterred. “Love is love,” she said. “Even if it comes in powdered form.”
